In which I adapt the solution to a [Stack Exchange problem](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/279711/counting-with-tensor-products) to be approachable to a general undergraduate math major audience, filling in the gaps to introduce tensor products, norms, generating functions, and Stirling's Formula.
